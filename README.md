# George A. Romero's Living Dead Series

## Overview

My favorite movie series is **George A. Romero's** zombie films, often called the *Dead* series. Romero is widely known as the father of the modern zombie movie. :zombie:

| ---- | ----- | ------- |

## Why I Like It

- **Horror with a message:** the zombies are scary, but the films are also about society
- **Practical effects:** the gore was done by hand, without CGI
- **Influence:** it shaped the zombie movies and shows that came after

> The real danger in these movies is often not the zombies, but the people trying to survive.
